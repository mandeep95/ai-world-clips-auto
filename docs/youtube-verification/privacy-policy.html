Privacy Policy — AI World Clips Auto

Last updated: 17 November 2026

AI World Clips Auto is a private automation that uploads one short video a day to the YouTube channel AI World Clips (@AIWorldClips-z4v), owned by the same person who runs this app. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API v3 with the single OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. With that scope it can only upload videos to the channel that granted access. It does not read video lists, comments, subscribers, watch history, or any other Google account data, and it does not request any other scope.

What data the app stores

  • A Google OAuth refresh token for the AI World Clips channel, stored as an encrypted secret in the private settings of the app's own GitHub repository. It is used only to mint short-lived access tokens for uploads.
  • The video files, titles and descriptions being uploaded — the channel owner's own content, stored in the same repository.

No data about any other person is collected, stored, or shared. The app has no website visitors, no analytics, and no third-party trackers.

What data the app shares

Nothing, except the video itself, which is uploaded to YouTube as a public Short on the owner's channel — exactly what the owner asked the app to do.

Data retention and deletion

The refresh token is kept until the owner revokes it. Anyone can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (third-party access). Revoking it immediately stops all uploads. The owner can also delete the stored token from the repository secrets at any time.
